## Hypothesis

Equal numbers of occupied classes, even equal class-size histograms, are not sufficient evidence of equivalent Boolean rule expression. This audit prevents false equivalence claims following expression compression. It is a verification criterion, not a new filtering rule.

For partitions A and B on the same nonempty finite set, let J be the number of occupied joint labels (a,b). Every A block contains at least one joint block. Thus J >= |A|, with equality iff each A block belongs to exactly one B block, i.e. A refines B. Symmetrically for B. Therefore the partitions induce the same Boolean algebra iff J = |A| = |B|. Necessity and sufficiency follow from the fact that Boolean algebras generated by finite partitions consist of unions of their blocks, with blocks as atoms.

Counterexample: on three synthetic elements, labels A=(0,0,1) and B=(0,1,0) each have two classes of sizes 1 and 2, but three occupied joint classes. Their generated Boolean algebras are distinct. A relabeling (0,0,1) to (1,1,0) is equivalent, despite distinct raw labels.
